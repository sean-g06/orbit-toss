ORBITTOSS

Nine holes of gravity golf. Time the throw, read the curve, sink the ball into the wormhole.

How to play

Two clicks per throw

  1. The arrow sweeps left and right. Click (or tap, or press Space) to lock the direction.
  2. The power bar fills and empties. Click again to lock the power and throw. The arrow grows with it so you can see the full throw.
  3. Gravity bends the ball. Land it in the glowing wormhole. Arrive too fast and it skims straight over.

The dotted ring round the wormhole is its gravity well: it pulls the ball in and slows it down. If the ball settles on a planet, your next throw starts from there. If it drifts off the map or flies for more than 12 seconds, it returns to where you threw from and the throw still counts.
